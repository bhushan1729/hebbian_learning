To ensure full reproducibility of all empirical findings and theoretical proofs:
\begin{enumerate}

    \item \textbf{Code \& Benchmark Artifacts}: All benchmarking scripts, model training pipelines, pruning utilities, and evaluation scripts will be released publicly upon publication.
\end{enumerate}
