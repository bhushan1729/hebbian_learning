\subsection*{Ethics Statement}

This work introduces an algorithmic method for deep neural network compression. All datasets used in this study (MNIST, CIFAR-10, CoNLL-2003, SST-2) are standard, publicly available benchmarks. The research does not involve human subjects, personal data privacy risks, or specialized environmental harms. By reducing compute and energy requirements during training and inference, network pruning contributes positively to sustainable and accessible artificial intelligence deployment.
